# Risk Register

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Technology stack not yet finalised | Medium | Medium | Confirm technologies after team discussion and client consultation |
| Healthcare datasets may be difficult to understand or prepare | Medium | High | Begin with simplified or synthetic datasets |
| Multimodal scope may become too large | Medium | High | Build a simple single-modality MVP before expanding |
| Different technical skill levels across team members | High | Medium | Allocate work according to strengths and pair on unfamiliar areas |
| Feature importance may be difficult to explain to school students | Medium | Medium | Prioritise simple visual explanations and usability testing |
